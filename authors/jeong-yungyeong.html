Illustrator of Montafon Moonlight

Biography

Illustrator Jeong Yungyeong was born in Seoul, South Korea. After graduating in sculpture from Kyungwon University, she pursued graduate studies in illustration at Kingston University in England. Through gentle, simple lines and colors, she seeks to connect with people everywhere. She illustrated The Joy of Non-Possession and Venerable Beopjeong’s Songs of Encouragement for Life, and her picture books include A Vessel for the Heart and The Fool Novice. She currently runs an art academy in Seoul and provides illustrations for the online Buddhist newspaper Media Buddha.
